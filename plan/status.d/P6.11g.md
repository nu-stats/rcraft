- **Done** (`crates/catalog/src/meta.rs`; `crates/engine/tests/meta.rs`; `plan/small-fixes.md` row 30):
  - `covariance(fixed(matname))` for `meta mvregress`: the Model box has "Random effects, known between-study covariance" and a box for the matrix's numbers in Stata's order (v11 v12 ... v1p v22 ... vpp). It reuses the `random(jwriley)` fit (`rma.mv` with `tau2` and `rho` fixed), so nothing is iterated; the matrix must be positive semidefinite (an error in R otherwise) and the structure box must stay at its default. Equal to Stata to 1e-7 on two outcomes with and without the year, three outcomes, an outcome missing in two studies, and with `se(jriley)` (scale 2.368293, t tests on 26 degrees of freedom). Not 1e-9: Stata's own coefficient solve stops a hair short of the exact GLS (2e-9 relative with the centred data, 1.4e-8 with the uncentred year), and the dialog gives the exact one.
  - The `se(jriley)` scale with a within-study covariance missing at a missing outcome (small-fixes row 30): Stata reads the missing covariance as 0, which is what the dialog did. Checked by probes with a known and with a Jackson-White-Riley between-study covariance, in seven variants (the covariance missing in study 3, 8 or both where the second outcome is missing, in studies 2 and 9 where the first is, with and without the year) and REML with a missing variance (1.00512387); all equal to Stata (1e-7 for the exact ones, 5e-5 for REML). When the variance of the missing outcome is missing too, the covariance does not matter (the variance is read as 1e6). The figure 1.1497 of the earlier probe could not be recreated and none of the variants gives it.
  - Random slopes off the boundary: a third made-up data set `md3` (48 studies in 12 labs, a lab's intercept and slope on a centred moderator correlated 0.6; plain `rnorm` draws, `set.seed(2611)`). Stata's `meta meregress y xc || lab: xc, covariance(unstructured) || sid:` puts the correlation at 0.859 and the dialog equals it coefficient by coefficient (the coefficients, the three standard deviations, the correlation and the log-likelihood, to 5e-5), with the independent fit beside it.
  - Random slopes at the outer levels and `noconstant`: a "Random slopes at the outer clusters" box (the slopes at every outer level chosen) and two boxes, "No random intercept with the slopes at the clusters / at the outer clusters" (the slopes alone, `x - 1`). `metafor` reports the second term with slopes as `gamma2` and `phi`, which the output prints beside the first. Equal to Stata to 5e-5 for a slope at the clusters without an intercept (REML and ML), at the region with the clusters' intercept, at the region and the clusters, at the region without an intercept, and at the clusters without an intercept under a region intercept; the log-likelihoods too.
  - Exchangeable and identity covariance of a level's slopes and intercept: built, not refused. metafor has no structure for them with a numeric slope, so the dialog finds the common log standard deviation (and atanh correlation, exchangeable) by maximising the likelihood of `rma.mv` with them fixed (`optim`, the other variances estimated inside each fit; a few seconds). Equal to Stata to 5e-5 with one slope (REML and ML) and two slopes (the correlation's atanh is Stata's for three random effects too), also when the structured level is the second term (a region slope without its intercept above). It works for one level at a time: slopes with an intercept (or two slopes) at two levels, both exchangeable or identity, is refused with a message.
  - The manual's smoking-cessation example of `estat heterogeneity` (`smokecess`, 24 studies, 31 of 72 values given; downloaded 2026-11-27 with the user's go, kept outside the repository after a search of every repository found no copy): `meta mvregress y*, wcovvariables(v*) random(mle, covariance(exchangeable))`.
