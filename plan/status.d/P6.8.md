## P6.8: resampling dialogs, 2026-11-23

- **Done** (`crates/catalog/src/resampling.rs`, `crates/engine/tests/resampling.rs`, one test per dialog, run in R 4.4.3 with boot 1.3.31, no new packages; `coin` is not used): both entries are Verified and in `stats::SPECS`.
  - Bootstrap: the row draws are `matrix(sample.int(n, n * R, replace = TRUE), R, n)` after `set.seed`, and equal `boot.array`'s; every replicate (mean, median, correlation, three regression coefficients) is recomputed by hand from them to 1e-9; the percentile limits are `quantile(type = 6)` (the 25th smallest and largest of 999); the normal limits are the bias-corrected estimate plus or minus z standard errors; the BCa limits are the jackknife acceleration and bias-correction constant interpolated on the normal scale, to 1e-9 for the mean.
  - Permutation test: on 8 rows (70 arrangements) the exact p-values for means and medians equal an independent listing by bit masks; on 6 rows (720) the exact correlation p-value equals a listing of all index tuples; the Monte Carlo p-value equals the same shuffles redone with `sample`; on Stata's 20 rows the exact p over all 184756 arrangements is 0.1259, Stata's `permute` gives 0.1288 (95% interval 0.1258 to 0.1317) and the dialog 0.1254 with B = 50000; for the correlation Stata gives 0.1523 (0.1492 to 0.1555) and the dialog 0.1549 (the test allows the Monte Carlo error of both).
- **Differences from Stata** (each in the test header): Stata's two-sided p is 2 * min(lower, upper), the dialog's counts the shuffles at least as large in size as the observed statistic and adds the observed arrangement ((count + 1)/(B + 1)); the two agree here within the Monte Carlo error. Stata's `permute` has no exact listing.
