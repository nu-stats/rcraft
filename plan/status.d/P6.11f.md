The estimate is the manual's: the matrices W (inverse within-study covariances), R (which outcomes are given), H (the fixed-effects hat matrix), P = (I - H)'W and B = (I - H)'R, then vec(Q) = vec(sum of B_jj) + (sum over j and l of B_jl' (x) P_lj) vec(Sigma), symmetrised and with negative eigenvalues cut to zero (Stata's note when it does so).
