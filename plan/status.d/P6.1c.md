## P6.1c: system GMM, instrument-lag limits, the small option, 2026-11-23

- **Done** (`crates/catalog/src/panel.rs`, `crates/engine/tests/panel.rs`; rows 7 and 8 of `plan/small-fixes.md` struck, 10 cut down):
  - System GMM: each unit is laid out on the whole grid of periods and stacks the difference equation (periods lags+2 to T; the outcome's levels from the first period to two back as instruments, a block for each period, and the differences of the other predictors as their own) and the level equation (periods max(3, lags+1) to T; the lagged difference of the outcome as the instrument); the first-step weight is block diagonal, the first-difference matrix over the identity. Coefficients, one-step conventional and robust errors, two-step conventional errors, Windmeijer's two-step robust errors and Sargan's statistic (one-step divided by sigma^2, the difference equation's residual sum of squares over twice its filled rows less the coefficients; two-step the quadratic form in the inverse score matrix) equal `xtdpdsys, noconstant` to 1e-14 on nine cases (lags 1 to 3; one and two exogenous predictors; maxldep 1 and 2).
  - The constant: Stata's `xtdpdsys` instruments it by itself in the level equation, which keeps the level row of the second period (its lagged-difference instrument does not exist yet, so without a constant that row carries nothing and is dropped). With that row the coefficients, all four error kinds and Sargan's statistic equal `xtdpdsys` to 1e-13 on six more cases.
  - Arellano and Bond's m1 and m2 after system GMM: the lagged residual is the difference equation's, padded with zeros over the level rows, while the residual it is weighted by is the whole system's. That last point is what the published formula leaves implicit; with it the statistics equal `e(arm1)` and `e(arm2)` to 1e-12 with and without a constant, after one step with robust errors and after two steps either way. After one step with conventional errors Stata leaves them out, and so does the dialog.
  - Time effects: a dummy for each period from the third on, among the exogenous predictors, differenced in the one equation and kept in the other. Equal to `xtdpdsys y x yr3-yr7` to 1e-15, with its Sargan and m1.
  - Units that miss periods: a row a unit cannot fill (a missing period, or a missing predictor) is zeroed and adds nothing to any sum. Equal to `xtdpdsys` on a panel with three rows dropped and one predictor missing, for the estimates, sigma^2, Sargan and all the errors. The time variable must still step evenly, which is checked.
  - Instrument-lag limit: Stata's `maxldep(q)` is plm's `lag(y, 2:(q+1))` in difference GMM (coefficients, errors, Sargan equal `xtabond` to 1e-9 for q = 1 to 3, both steps) and the last q lags in the difference equation of system GMM.
  - Panel IV `small`: t statistics with the degrees of freedom of `e(df_r)` in Stata (within N-G-K; unclustered random effects N-K+1 for its three columns; clustered random effects G; differences N-K, clustered G-1; between G-K).
- **Not built** (listed, to be built): robust errors after EC2SLS and panel IV's `nosa` and `regress` (both refused meanwhile; `plan/small-fixes.md` row 10 records how far each was traced). Difference GMM stays `noconstant` on purpose: `xtabond` with a constant reports no constant and the same estimates, and moves only the one-step conventional errors, by one degree of freedom.
