## P6.9c: Stata's own Bayesian priors, 2026-11-25

- **Done** (`crates/catalog/src/bayes.rs`, `crates/engine/tests/bayes.rs`, one new test, R 4.4.3 with rstanarm 2.32.1's rstan for the summary table, no new packages):
  - A custom Stan model would need a C++ toolchain (Rtools) on every user's machine, so the fit is a random-walk Metropolis-Hastings sampler written in the dialog's R: started at the maximum-likelihood fit (`lm.fit`, `glm.fit`, `MASS::glm.nb`, `MASS::polr`) jittered by one standard error per chain, its proposal covariance and scale tuned during the warm-up (acceptance near 0.234), then fixed. It keeps a `bayes_mh` list (draws by iteration, chain and parameter, acceptance rates); the table is `rstan::monitor`'s, with rstanarm's columns and the same convergence warning.
  - Decoded from Stata's output on made-up data: `bayes: glm, family(gamma)` fixes the scale at 1 in the likelihood (it prints the ML scale, but its posterior spread is that of scale 1); `bayes: ologit` puts flat priors on the cutpoints; `bayes: nbreg` normal(0, 10000) on log alpha.
  - Gaussian (40 rows): with Stata's defaults, and with normal(0, 25) slopes, the means lie within four MCSEs of the exact posterior (a grid over sigma-squared), as Stata's do, and within four combined MCSEs of Stata's with no slack; sds within 10%. Logit with defaults, Poisson with a Student t slope and normal(0, 2) constant (also the exact grid posterior), negative binomial, Gamma and ordered logit with defaults: within four combined MCSEs of Stata's, no slack (the ordered logit was 0.3 posterior sd off under rstanarm's Dirichlet cutpoints). The same seed gives the same draws; an outcome the family cannot take is an error.
- **Difference that stays**: log alpha's posterior under normal(0, 10000), on counts with little overdispersion, has a long tail toward minus infinity (about a fifth of its mass below -15 on the test data, by a Laplace step); random-walk samplers, Stata's and this one, stay near the mode, so neither mean is the posterior's. The dialog's about text says so; the coefficients barely depend on it. The fit is not an rstanarm fit, so the Model menu does not take it.
