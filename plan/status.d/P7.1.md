- P7.1 Publication tables, second half (done 2026-11-24): the Model table takes extra rows (a box with one row per line, written `label | value for the first model | value for the next`, put under the statistics block, a short row padded with blanks) and models of other classes side by side: `lm`, `glm`, `fixest`, `plm`, `coxph`, `survreg`, `svyglm`, `lmerMod`/`glmerMod`, `polr` and `multinom`. Base R, no new package.

## P7.1: publication tables, second half, 2026-11-24

- **Done** (`crates/catalog/src/report.rs`, new `TextRule::Lines` in `crates/rcodegen/src/dialog.rs` with a multi-line box in `crates/ui-egui/src/spec_dialog.rs`, `crates/engine/tests/model_table.rs`, run in R 4.4.3, no new packages): `custom_rows_go_under_the_statistics` and `other_model_classes_sit_side_by_side` are live; with `robust_errors_for_every_class_that_has_them`, `r_squared_for_fixest_and_plm_and_labelled_coefficients` and `mixed_and_panel_models_reach_the_model_table`; the existing `the_model_table_is_the_one_built_by_hand` and `an_exported_table_holds_every_cell` still pass unchanged.
  - One table holds an `lm`, a `coxph`, a `feols`, a `survreg`, a `polr`, a `multinom`, a `svyglm`, a `plm` and a `lmer` model; every cell equals the model's `coef` (or `fixef`) and `sqrt(diag(vcov))`.
  - P-values (stars, statistics and intervals) use the t distribution for `lm` and `plm` and for a `glm` whose dispersion is estimated, the normal for the others.
  - Robust errors for every class that has them: lm and glm (`sandwich::vcovHC`, the HC type chosen; equal to the HC1 sandwich written out), plm (`plm::vcovHC`, clustered on the unit; equal to Arellano's sandwich by hand), fixest (its heteroskedasticity-robust errors, HC1 only), coxph and survreg (refitted with `robust = TRUE`; equal to the sum of squared dfbeta residuals), polr (`sandwich::sandwich`) and multinom (scores by hand, since sandwich has none for it). Survey and mixed models are refused with a message.
  - R-squared and adjusted R-squared for fixest (the whole model's, by hand) and plm (the within one, by hand, with plm's adjustment); blank for the classes that have none.
  - Coefficient order and labels: a box with one coefficient per line, `term | label`; those listed come first in that order, under their labels.
  - The Objects pane offers the Model table on `fixest` and `plm` models (it was hidden for them), and lme4's `lmerMod` and `glmerMod` now have a Model menu (Model fit and the Model table).
- **Not built** (listed, to be built): clustered errors on a variable the user chooses.
