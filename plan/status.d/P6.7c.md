## P6.7c: multivariate leftovers, 2026-11-24

- **Done** (`crates/catalog/src/multivariate.rs`, `crates/engine/tests/multivariate.rs`, three new tests, R 4.4.3 with MASS 7.3.65, no new packages):
  - Principal factors in base R: the eigenvectors of the correlation matrix with the squared multiple correlations (pf, ipf) or ones (pcf) on its diagonal, scaled by the root eigenvalues and signed so each column sums above 0. ipf puts the communalities back until their squared changes sum below 1e-8 (Stata's stop, found by observation: 23 rounds for two factors, 3642 for three, where a uniqueness ends below 0 as in Stata). Only eigenvalues above 0 (above 1 for pcf) give factors, so pf with 4 asked keeps 3 and pcf with 3 asked keeps 2, as Stata. Loadings, uniquenesses and eigenvalues equal `factor, pf | ipf | pcf` to 1e-9 (1, 2 and 3 factors, five and six variables).
  - Varimax: Kaiser's exact angle for two factors, the SVD iteration (`stats::varimax`) stopped at a relative change of 1e-8 for more, as Stata's `rotate, varimax` (to 1e-12 and 1e-10); "to the exact maximum" sweeps the pairs until every angle is below 1e-15 (a higher criterion than Stata's, about 1e-4 away at three factors).
  - Promax: the varimax loadings raised to the power (Stata's default 3) as the target, the least-squares fit normalised to unit factor variances; loadings and factor correlations equal `rotate, promax[()]` to 1e-10, after principal, principal-component and maximum-likelihood factors (the last to 1e-6, the fit's own agreement).
  - Oblimin (oblique, gamma typed, 0 for quartimin): gradient projection from the unrotated factors, stopped when the projected gradient is below 1e-8; a minimum of the criterion (no small turn of an axis lowers it) and `rotate, oblimin() oblique` at tolerance 1e-13 to 1e-7 (Stata's default stop within 1e-5). Oblique factors are ordered by the column sums of squares of the structure matrix and signed by their sums, as Stata; their correlations print and stay in `name$Phi`.
  - Principal components "sign as Stata": each component signed so its loadings sum above 0; loadings and scores equal `pca` to 1e-9.
  - Clusters: every merge height equals Lance and Williams on the matrices built in the test (L1, L-infinity, Canberra as Stata's |a - b| / (|a| + |b|), squared Euclidean with average linkage, Euclidean with Ward's); the six largest heights and the three groups are Stata's. k-means under L1 and L-infinity (nearest centre, the first on ties; centres the means) equals Lloyd's loop by hand and Stata's groups. Stopping rules (`name$rules`): Calinski-Harabasz for 2 to the asked number and Duda-Hart Je(2)/Je(1) with pseudo-T-squared for each split, equal to `cluster stop, rule(calinski | duda)` to 1e-9 after Ward's and average-L1 linkage, and Calinski-Harabasz after k-means.
  - Quadratic discriminant: the posteriors are each group's own normal density (covariance over n - 1) times the prior, by hand, and equal `discrim qda` with `predict double, pr` to 1e-12 with equal and proportional priors; the classification is Stata's row for row.
- **Differences from Stata**: Stata's `predict` makes float variables unless asked for double (the probabilities then agree to about 1e-8 only).
