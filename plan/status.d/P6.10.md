## P6.10: Structural equation models, 2026-11-25

- **Done** (`crates/catalog/src/sem.rs`; `lavaan` wired in `postest.rs`; `crates/engine/tests/sem.rs`; lavaan in `ci.yml`):
  - Stata's `sem` found by running both: intercepts estimated (`meanstructure = TRUE`), errors from the observed information (Stata's OIM; lavaan's default expected information is about 1e-3 off), listwise deletion on the model's variables. Observed exogenous variables: Stata holds their means, variances and covariances at the sample values (divided by N) yet lets them covary freely with latent exogenous variables, which lavaan's default (`fixed.x = TRUE`) keeps at zero (chi-squared 48.2 on 13 against Stata's 11.88 on 12 on the test data). The dialog adds those covariances and fixes the exogenous moments at the sample values, marked as lavaan's own fixed exogenous moments so the degrees of freedom, log-likelihood (which then includes the exogenous variables, as Stata's), AIC and BIC are Stata's.
  - Equal to Stata 19 on 300 made-up rows, with and without missing values: estimates and errors to 5e-6 (Stata stops about 5e-7 short of the maximum; small-fixes row 24), chi-squared, log-likelihood, AIC, BIC, CFI and TLI to 1e-9, SRMR to 1e-5, standardised estimates and their errors (Stata's `standardized`, lavaan's Std.all) to 5e-6. RMSEA is Stata's on N - 1 and SRMR leaves the means out, as `estat gof`; lavaan's summary prints its own (on N, with the means), so the dialog prints Stata's after it (small-fixes row 25). With the box off, the fit is `lavaan::sem(model, data)` exactly.
