## P6.9: Bayesian regression, 2026-11-23

- **Done** (`crates/catalog/src/bayes.rs`, `crates/engine/tests/bayes.rs`, three tests, run in R 4.4.3 with rstanarm 2.32.1; rstanarm added to the `ci.yml` package list): the entry `Bayesian regression` is Verified and in `stats::SPECS`.
  - Exact answer: for a Gaussian model with normal priors on the intercept and slopes, the posterior of the coefficients given sigma is normal and the data's density given sigma is normal, so each posterior mean is a one-dimensional integral over sigma (done on a grid, using the priors rstanarm reports). The sampler's means (4 chains of 2000, seed 1) lie within four Monte Carlo standard errors of it (observed: 0.4 to 2 errors); R-hat is below 1.02, n_eff above 500, the intervals are the draws' own quantiles, and the same seed gives the same draws.
  - Stata 19 `bayes: regress` (normal(0,25) on the slopes, its default priors otherwise, 25,000 iterations): Stata's own means equal the exact answer under Stata's priors to within 1.6 of its MCSEs, and the dialog's agree with Stata's to within three combined MCSEs plus a tenth of a posterior sd (the intercept and error-scale priors differ between the programs).
  - Logit and Poisson: posterior means with a normal(0, 10) prior lie within a quarter of a posterior sd (plus Monte Carlo error) of `glm`'s estimates; a logit model for a continuous outcome returns an error and R stays up; the logit and Poisson fits use a normal(0, 100) prior.
- **Differences from Stata** (in `plan/small-fixes.md`): rstanarm's priors for the intercept and the error scale differ from Stata's defaults and cannot be made the same (the dialog's text says so); the dialog reports sigma where Stata reports sigma2; Stata's `bayes` uses random-walk Metropolis-Hastings, so draws differ and only means within Monte Carlo error are comparable. Fixed: a very diffuse prior (sd 100) on a Poisson model failed to converge (R-hat above 3); the chains now start nearer zero (`init_r = 0.5`), and a warning follows the table when R-hat is above 1.05 or n_eff below 100.
- **Not built** (listed, to be built): other families (negative binomial, ordinal, Gamma), a different prior for each coefficient or the intercept, other prior families (Student t, Cauchy, lasso), posterior predictive checks and trace plots, model comparison (loo), the Model menu (predictions from a stanreg fit).
