  - Found by running both: Stata's `meta mvregress` keeps a study with an outcome missing (26 observations in 14 studies on the test data), so the dialog leaves out only that outcome's row of the long table and the matching row and column of the study's within-study block; a missing moderator leaves the study out (24 observations in 13 studies); an outcome given with its variance or a covariance missing is an error in both. `wsevariables(se1 ... sep) wcorrelations(r12 r13 ... r23 ...)` builds each block as S R S, one correlation matrix for all studies (Stata requires the correlations); the dialog's one within-study box takes either p standard errors or the p(p + 1)/2 variances and covariances, which differ in number for two or more outcomes.
  - Stata 19's `meta mvregress` has no Knapp-Hartung adjustment (`se(khartung)` is refused, also inside `random()`); its one adjustment is `random(reml, se(jriley))`: each coefficient's variance times the residuals' weighted sum of squares (with the marginal covariances) over N - p, the covariances unscaled, t tests on N - p degrees of freedom, and the F test of the moderators on the model's own errors. The dialog fits with `test = "t", dfs = "residual"` and `btt` on the moderators, then scales the variances; equal to Stata to 1e-6 with every outcome given (5e-6 with an uncentred moderator). With outcomes missing, Stata's scale differs (row 30).
  - `estat heterogeneity` after `meta mvregress`: Cochran's H² is Q over N - p and I² = 1 - 1/H² (to 1e-9); Jackson, White and Riley's R for an outcome is (|V_R| / |V_F|)^(1/(2q)) over that outcome's q coefficients, the random-effects against the fixed-effects covariance, and jointly over all coefficients, with I² = 1 - 1/R² (to 1e-6; 5e-5 for the joint R with a moderator). With every outcome given, Stata prints the joint R in the first outcome's row (with outcomes missing it prints the outcome's own, which the dialog equals); the dialog shows each outcome's own (row 31). `estat recovariance` is the fit's G (to 1e-6, 2e-5 with three outcomes). The figures stay as `<name>_het`.
  - `meta multilevel est, relevels(reg lab sid)` is `rma.mv(random = ~ 1 | reg/lab/.study)` (to 1e-6; the lab variance is at zero in both), `mle` equals ML (1e-5: a small variance on a flat likelihood), and `meta meregress est year || lab: || sid:` is the same with `mods` (5e-6 with the uncentred year).
  - Forest-plot columns draw through `metafor::forest(ilab =, ilab.lab =)`, also with a study left out and with subgroups.
