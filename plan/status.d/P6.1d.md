  - Decoded on made-up panels (30 units, 6 periods, and the same with 7 rows dropped): sigma_e^2 is the within 2SLS residual sum of squares over N - n - K, or N - n with `nosa`; sigma_u^2 the adapted Swamy-Arora (sum of T_i e_i^2 - (n - K - 1) sigma_e^2) / (N - tr((X'PX)^-1 X'PPX)), with e the between 2SLS residuals and X the regressors with the constant, or with `nosa` (sum of T_i e_i^2 - n sigma_e^2) / N; theta_i = 1 - sqrt(sigma_e^2 / (T_i sigma_u^2 + sigma_e^2)).
