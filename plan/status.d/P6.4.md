Survey ratios (`survey::svyratio`, overall or for each group, as Stata's `svy: ratio`) and Survey quantiles (`survey::svyquantile`, the smallest value whose weighted cumulative share reaches the probability, with Woodruff intervals; Stata has no matching command, so the dialog's help states the rule).
