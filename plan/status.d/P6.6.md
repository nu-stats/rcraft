## P6.6: IV and selection, second half, 2026-11-25

- **Done** (`crates/catalog/src/iv.rs`; `selection` wired in `postest.rs`; `crates/engine/tests/iv.rs`):
  - LIML: kappa is the smallest eigenvalue of (Y'M_Z Y)^-1 Y'M_1 Y, the coefficients the k-class ones; the conventional variance s^2 (X'(I - k M_Z) X)^-1, the robust and clustered ones with that bread and the 2SLS-projected regressors in the meat (found by matching Stata, not assumed). Equal to `ivregress liml` to 1e-9 with conventional, robust and clustered errors, each with and without `small` (N - K; N / (N - K); G / (G - 1) (N - 1) / (N - K); no factor without small), and kappa to 1e-12; kappa is also the least variance ratio found by `optimize` written out in the test.
  - GMM: two steps, the 2SLS residuals weighting the moments (robust, or clustered when a cluster is chosen, as `wmatrix(cluster)`); the variance is the sandwich with the second step's residuals, or N (X'Z W Z'X)^-1 for Conventional, which is Stata's `vce(unadjusted)`; `small` multiplies by N / (N - K) or the cluster factor. Hansen's J uses the first step's weights, as `estat overid`. Coefficients, errors and J equal Stata's to 1e-9, also with no exogenous predictor.
  - IV probit or tobit: the joint likelihood of the outcome and the endogenous predictor in Stata's parameters (beta, gamma, atanh rho, log sigma), with analytic scores; `optim` (BFGS) and then Newton steps with the Hessian from `optimHess` on the scores at step 1e-5 (optimHess's default 1e-3 is 6e-5 off Stata's errors). Log-likelihoods equal Stata's to 1e-12; errors (observed information, robust with N / (N - 1), clustered with G / (G - 1)) and the Wald test of exogeneity to 1e-6. Stata's `ivprobit` stops about 1e-7 short of the maximum the dialog reaches on this flat likelihood, so estimates are held to 1e-6 (the tobit's agree to 1e-8). Tobit with `ll(0)`, `ul(10)` and both; corr and the standard deviations with the delta method's errors and transformed intervals, as Stata prints them.
  - Treatment effects: the outcome's linear model in each group and the treatment's logit by `glm.fit`, then the effects solved from the stacked estimating equations and Stata's robust variance as their sandwich (numerical Jacobian, about 1e-10). ATE, ATET and the potential-outcome means for RA, IPW (normalised weights) and AIPW equal `teffects` to 1e-9, with different outcome and treatment predictors and with none; point estimates also written out with `lm` and weighted means.
  - Model menu: `selection` takes Model fit (observations, log-likelihood, AIC, BIC as Stata's `estat ic`); a two-step fit says it has no likelihood. LIML, GMM, IV probit or tobit and treatment-effects results are plain lists (coefficients, vcov, nobs), not on the Model menu.
