- **Done** (`crates/catalog/src/panel.rs`, `crates/engine/tests/panel.rs`, R 4.4.3, no new packages): `panel_iv_nosa_regress_and_ec2sls_robust_match_xtivreg` checks the unbalanced panel (seven rows dropped) to 1e-9 against Stata 19 for `xtivreg, re`, `re vce(robust)`, `re ec2sls`, `re ec2sls vce(robust)`, `re ec2sls nosa` and `re ec2sls nosa vce(robust)` (coefficients, variances, a covariance, sigma_u, sigma_e), and the small option's degrees of freedom.
  - EC2SLS's instruments, decoded by black-box runs on made-up unbalanced panels: for each exogenous variable and instrument, its within deviation with the grand mean (over the estimation rows) added back, and its unit mean; for the constant only 1 - theta_i, no plain constant. Clues: the `first` option's first stage names a deviation part, a mean part and a constant; a purely within instrument gives the G2SLS estimate exactly; shifting an instrument by a constant changes the estimate (so no plain constant is among the instruments). On a balanced panel theta is one number and the set spans the plain within and between parts, as before. The [XT] manual's Methods and formulas describe the set only loosely.
  - The small option (verified for G2SLS, EC2SLS, `nosa`, `regress`, robust or not): the errors do not change; t statistics on N - p + 1 degrees of freedom, or n units with robust errors. It is no longer refused with the direct fit.
  - Random effects no longer go through plm (whose unbalanced Swamy-Arora components and EC2SLS instruments are not Stata's); the fit is saved as a list of coefficients, covariance and components. Fixed effects, between and first differences still use plm.
