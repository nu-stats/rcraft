# P6.11 Meta-analysis (open → done) · 2 h · install metafor (decision 1)

**Build:** Statistics > Meta-analysis, in a new `crates/catalog/src/meta.rs`.

**The dialog:**
- effect sizes from the data (`escalc`: SMD, log OR, log RR, or a given estimate and SE);
- fixed or random effects (REML or DerSimonian-Laird);
- forest and funnel plots;
- Egger's test optional.

**R:** `metafor::rma`, `forest`, `funnel`, `regtest`.

**Stata 19 check:**
- `meta set` or `meta esize`, then `meta summarize, random(reml)` and `random(dlaird)`: pooled estimate, τ², I²;
- `meta bias, egger`.

Add metafor to `ci.yml`.

**Test:** add `crates/engine/tests/meta.rs`.
